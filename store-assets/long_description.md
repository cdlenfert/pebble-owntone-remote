OwnTone Remote

Control your OwnTone (owntone/owntone) music server from your Pebble watch. Browse results, play/pause, skip tracks, and adjust volume or outputs without touching your phone.

Features
- Search and browse results
- Play, pause, next, previous
- Adjust volume and switch outputs
- Favorite items and quick access

How to use
1. Open the app on your Pebble.
2. Pair with your phone and configure the server address in the mobile configuration page.
3. Use the UI to browse results and control playback.

Privacy
No personal data is sent to third-party services; the app communicates directly with your OwnTone server on your local network.
